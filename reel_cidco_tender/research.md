# CIDCO plot tender / e-auction (research 10 Oct 2026)
- CIDCO does not sell land: plots are LEASED (usually 60 yrs; verify per booklet) via e-tender + e-auction. Uses: residential, commercial, R+C, bungalow, service industry, warehouse.
- Portal: eauction.cidcoindia.com -> Bidder Registration (name, address, PAN, Aadhaar, bank IFSC).
- Steps: download scheme booklet -> pay document fee (earlier schemes Rs 1,000 + 18% GST = Rs 1,180) + EMD (varies by plot) by closed-bid deadline via RTGS/NEFT (EMD validated before bidding) -> closed bid (e-tender) -> live e-auction, increments Rs 1,000 per sq m -> highest bid wins.
- Winner's EMD adjusted in price; others refunded. Withdraw within 3 months of offer -> EMD forfeited. Instalment schedule per booklet (do not quote 25/75 - that's RIICO).
- Extra costs not in bid: GST, stamp duty, registration, lease rent, taxes, deposits. Possession within 30 days after full payment. Use of plot cannot be changed; owner gets own approvals.
- Recent: Scheme MM/SCH-47/2025-26: 20 plots, e-tender 2-22 Dec 2025, e-auction 23 Dec 2025. Aug-Sep 2025: 33 plots Airoli/Ghansoli/Sanpada/Nerul/Belapur, 63.05 to 10,917.77 sq m. 2024: 48 plots, base Rs 90,000 to 3 lakh per sq m; bids reportedly 3-4x base.
- Near-airport auction (late 2025): Kharghar bungalow plots reserve Rs 1.25 lakh/sq m; 41,994 sq m mixed-use plot base Rs 3.51 lakh/sq m; highest bid Rs 5.06 lakh/sq m (Aakar Astron) ~ Rs 2,125 crore (eauctionsindia; urbanacres). VERIFY before using as hook.
