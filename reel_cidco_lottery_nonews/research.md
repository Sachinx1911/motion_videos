## CIDCO booklet Dec 2025 (cidcohomes.com, local text copy cidco_booklet_dec2025.txt)
- EWS income up to ₹6 lakh; LIG "more than ₹6 lakhs ... There is no upper income limit for LIG category." (lines 319-322)
- Booking amount: EWS ₹75,000; LIG 1 BHK ₹1,50,000; LIG 2 BHK ₹2,00,000 (lines 532-533, 679)
- Unsuccessful applicants: booking amount refunded (line 545)
- Cancel after confirmation letter / LOI: refund minus ₹5,000 + GST (lines 737-739)
- Forfeiture: booking amount forfeited if successful applicant withdraws/surrenders after Letter of Allotment (754-757); after allotment letter surrender -> total booking amount + 10% of instalments paid (excl. confirmation amount) forfeited (761-766)
- Floor rise LIG: ₹10 per sq ft per floor from 6th floor (751-752); none for EWS
- LIG prices in table: ₹30.80 lakh (Taloja Sec 37 LIG-A) to ₹87.50 lakh (Kharghar Station Sec 1A LIG-B) (lines 156-168)
- 10% price cut on EWS/LIG (line 34, 52)
