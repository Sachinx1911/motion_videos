# Parking charges - what builder can and cannot charge (research 17 Oct 2026)
- SC Nahalchand Laloochand v Panchali CHS (2010) 9 SCC 536, AIR 2010 SC 3607: under MOFA, stilt/open parking = common area, not a flat/garage; promoter cannot sell it; only a garage (roof + walls on 3 sides) is saleable. Even buyers' signed declarations could not override. Applies to MOFA-era (pre-RERA) deals and still governs MOFA.
- RERA era (Maharashtra): MahaRERA Circular 36/2021 - OPEN parking = common area, cannot be charged at all. Garage and COVERED parking (basement, stilt, podium, mechanised, MLCP if approved as covered) CAN be charged, but only as exclusive-use allotment attached to the flat - not a separate immovable unit; cannot be sold/transferred/mortgaged separately.
- MahaRERA Order 54/2024: allotment letter + agreement for sale must state parking type (garage/covered/open), number, dimensions (length, width; height for covered), marked on approved layout plan.
- MahaRERA orders: Mahesh Shah v Sunny Vista Realtors / Persipina (20 Jan 2020) - Rs 4 lakh charged for open parking, buyers exempted. Nandraj Developers (Royal Residency C, Lalbaug) refunded Rs 25,000 open parking charge.
- Remedy: complaint on maharera.mahaonline.gov.in; refund with interest possible. After society registration, unallotted parking belongs to society.
- SCRIPT CARE: do NOT say "stilt parking cannot be charged" for RERA projects. Say: OPEN parking - never. Covered/stilt/basement - only if written in agreement with number + size; otherwise illegal; never a separate "sale".
