## Internal inconsistencies / risks
1. Lonavala: A 1.1 lists Lonavala as NOT covered, but B 5.9 gives Lonavala-Karla rules and B mentions a Pune RP (Lonavala) table. Need exact wording of 1.1 (council area vs RP area).
2. 300 sq.ft = 27.87 sq.m (C 7.6.1) vs 27.88 sq.m (D 14.3, E 14.6). Use the figure printed in each regulation; 300 sq.ft = 27.871 sq.m.
3. CC validity: A 2.7.1 (4 years aggregate, yearly renewal) vs F D-1 (valid 1 year if work not commenced). Not a contradiction, but F reel idea 5 "1 वर्षात परवानगी संपू शकते" is misleading without the renewal rule.
4. Height: B 6.10 cites amendment of 25 Sep 2024; C 10.1.1 Pune height-vs-road "ceased 1 Jan 2022, 2024 modification stayed" - status unclear, do not use for Pune height claims.
5. Summary copy is dated 30 Jan 2025; today is 8 Oct 2026. Any notification after 30 Jan 2025 is not covered - check latest amendments before each reel.
6. D +5% early TDR window (2 years from 2 Dec 2020) has expired - do not present as current.
7. D 14.1 township 50% stamp duty concession / property-tax 66% are linked to other State policies - verify current status separately.
8. F App. K Moderate-risk row split "inferred - verify"; App. K cites App. H for completion cert (G is completion, H is OC) - check original.
9. A def.74 high-rise ">= 24 m" vs C 10.2.10 Thane "25 m" - city-specific, mention city when used.
10. Several items are amounts/percentages from garbled tables (D hand-over %, C 8-B, E corpus slab) - never use on screen without the original page.
