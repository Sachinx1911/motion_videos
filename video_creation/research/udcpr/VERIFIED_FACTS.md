## Summary corrections found
- Summary A 1.1: "Lonavala" = Lonavala Municipal Council only.
- Summary A 2.8.3 / TOPICS: display board has 6 items, and not needed for an individual's own building.
- 300 sq.ft is printed as 27.87 sq.m in 7.6.1 / 7.6.2 (use 27.87 for redevelopment videos).
