# 79A / Redevelopment GR (30 Sept 2026) - research notes (2026-10-08)

Status: NEWS-LEVEL ONLY. Official GR text not seen yet. Do not lock facts until the GR PDF (Co-operation Department, gr.maharashtra.gov.in) is checked.

## Reported (Construction World, 6 Oct 2026)
- GR date 30 Sept 2026, Co-operation Department; replaces 4 July 2019 directives (s.79A, MCS Act 1960) + related circulars.
- Approval: at least 51% of TOTAL membership; SGM quorum two-thirds.
- Remote participation (abroad / ill / disabled / unavoidable) counts for quorum and majority; absent members' votes not counted.
- Written consent from members voting for redevelopment.
- Developer-selection meeting video-recorded; copy to society + registrar.
- Minimum 3 competitive tenders; if fewer, extend tender period.
- Architect / PMC: building + land assessment, FSI / TDR review, project report.
- Development Agreement within 3 months of developer selection; PAAA within 3 months of DA registration.
- All documents available to members free for inspection.
- Completion generally within 2 years of first / plinth certificate (max 3 years in exceptional cases).
- Action against office-bearers for violations / collusion / fraud.
- Not stated: bank guarantee, penalty amounts.

## Conflicts / unverified
- 20% bank guarantee (from 2019 GR per guides) - unknown in 2026 GR.
- "60% consent" claims in some blogs - no official basis found.
