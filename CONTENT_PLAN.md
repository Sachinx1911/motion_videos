# SRS Dwell - Content plan (22 Nov 2026)
Goal: build a follower base by teaching home buyers everything, step by step: Phase 1 basics -> Phase 2 design/colour -> Phase 3 interiors.
Every reel: 1 idea, 55-65 s, hook in 2 s, comment CTA + follow. Languages: Marathi / Hindi / English (one build, 3 voices where the topic fits all).

## Phase 1 - "घर घेण्याआधी" basics (12-16 weeks, 3 reels/week)
Series: DOCUMENT 101 · QUICK MATH · TRUE CASE · CHECKLIST
1. Flat types: 1BHK/2BHK, studio, duplex, row house, villa - what you actually get
2. Carpet vs built-up vs super built-up (loading %) - QUICK MATH
3. RERA: what it is, what it guarantees (carpet, possession date, 70% escrow, refund+interest)
4. How to read a MahaRERA project page (registration no., promoter, dates, complaints)
5. Plot area: sq ft / sq m / guntha / acre, FSI, what you can build (UDCPR intro exists)
6. NA plot vs agricultural plot, 7/12, 8A, ferfar
7. Index II, Index I search (exists - Marathi; make Hindi/English)
8. Ready Reckoner rate and stamp duty + registration cost (exists - Marathi)
9. OC / CC / completion - why no OC = no water, no loan, no resale
10. Agreement to Sale: 7 clauses to read (possession date, penalty/week, carpet, parking, defect liability, amenities, cancellation)
11. Booking amount, token, cancellation refund rules
12. Home loan: eligibility, EMI math, 20/80 rule, pre-EMI, CIBIL
13. Resale flat checklist: chain of agreements, society NOC, dues, share certificate
14. Society basics: conveyance / deemed conveyance, maintenance, sinking fund, transfer fee limit
15. Parking: who owns it (SC Nahalchand) - TRUE CASE
16. Possession delay: RERA refund with interest - TRUE CASE
17. Redevelopment series (exists: karar, tax, stalled, riddle HI)
18. CIDCO lottery / lease / tender (exists - Marathi; refresh when news comes)
19. TDR, cluster, UDCPR (exists)
20. New vs resale vs under-construction: cost table - QUICK MATH
21. GST on flats, TDS on property > 50 L, capital gains basics
22. Vastu basics vs practical planning (light, ventilation) - bridge to Phase 2

## Phase 2 - Design & colour (8-10 weeks)
Series: DESIGN 101 · COLOUR CARD · BEFORE/AFTER
1. Floor plan reading: how to judge a 2BHK plan in 60 s (dead space, door swings, kitchen triangle)
2. Room sizes that actually work (bed 10x12, living 12x16...)
3. Colour combinations by room: 5 palettes per room (living, bedroom, kids, kitchen, bathroom) - COLOUR CARD reels
4. Paint types and finishes, cost per sq ft, what lasts
5. Lighting plan: warm vs cool, 3-layer lighting, lux per room
6. Flooring: tiles vs marble vs wood vs vinyl - cost + care table
7. Small flat tricks: 1BHK that looks big (mirrors, light colours, built-ins)
8. Balcony, window grills, false ceiling - worth it or not
9. Design mistakes buyers regret (comments-driven)
Visual style: same engine; add real photo/render cut-outs, colour swatch cards drawn in code (approved: swatches are data, not props).

## Phase 3 - Interiors (ongoing)
Series: INTERIOR 101 · BUDGET BREAKDOWN · MATERIAL GUIDE
1. Interior budget for 1/2/3BHK in Navi Mumbai (ranges), what % goes where
2. Modular kitchen: carcass, shutters, hardware, cost table
3. Wardrobes: plywood vs MDF vs particle board, laminate vs acrylic vs veneer
4. Bathroom: fittings, waterproofing, ventilation
5. Furniture buying vs custom, timelines, contractor vs designer vs turnkey
6. Quotation reading: 10 hidden charges
7. Electrical plan: points per room, AC load, inverter
8. Maintenance: dampness, termite, warranty
9. Client stories / BEFORE-AFTER (needs real photos from SRS Dwell projects) - leads to business
