# Repair or redevelopment? 30 years != must demolish (research 27 Oct 2026)

## NMMC dangerous buildings 2026-27 (survey u/s 264 MMC Act, list ~22-23 May 2026)
- Total 504: C-1 52 (highly dangerous, uninhabitable, evacuate + demolish), C-2A 102 (vacate for structural repair), C-2B 297 (repair without vacating), C-3 53 (minor repair). 52+102+297+53 = 504.
- Sources: Free Press Journal 22 May 2026 (504, 52 C-1, 102 C-2A); PropNewsTime 23 May 2026 (all four). C-2B/C-3 split = PropNewsTime only -> voice uses "उरलेल्या" not exact 297/53.
- Aug 2026: C-1 public notice lists 33/34 buildings (sources differ) -> NOT used.
- 2025-26 for comparison: 501 (51 C-1).
- List on nmmc.gov.in, Encroachment Dept > General Information.

## Structural audit frequency
- Model bye-laws (2014) Bye-law 76(a) (old Bye-law 77): building 15-30 yrs -> structural audit once in 5 years; > 30 yrs -> once in 3 years; in Municipal Corporation areas by engineers from the corporation's panel. [Sahakar Ayukta model bye-laws PDF; Cubeone; adda.io]

## 30-year rule = eligibility, not compulsion
- UDCPR 10.10.2: CIDCO-built building above 30 years can be redeveloped "irrespective of its status of dilapidation" (research/redev_rules.md). Cluster 14.8: 30 yrs or dilapidated.

## Not used
- Koparkhairane ceiling collapse 25 Oct 2026 (Inshorts only, injury details) - single source, sensitive.
- Repair vs redevelopment cost figures - no reliable source.
