# Ready Reckoner rate (ASR) - research (18 Nov 2026)
Reel: "Ready Reckoner rate म्हणजे काय?" · SRS Dwell · educational

## What it is
- Maharashtra calls it the **Annual Statement of Rates (ASR)**, popularly "ready reckoner" (RR) / circle rate. Published by IGR (Inspector General of Registration & Stamps) every year, effective 1 April, per village/zone, separate rates for land, flat (residential), office, shop, industrial.
- Stamp duty and registration are charged on the **higher of agreement value and RR value**.
- Where to check: igrmaharashtra.gov.in -> e-ASR (1.9 / 2.0) -> year -> district -> taluka -> village/zone (-> survey no.). No official app; use the IGR site or the Sub-Registrar office.

## Current status
- **FY 2026-27: rates frozen (0% change)** across rural/urban/influence zones - state cited global uncertainty and construction costs (FPJ, Nagpur Today, Square Feat India, Mar-Apr 2026).
- FY 2025-26: state average +3.89%; Navi Mumbai about +6.7% (Business Standard 1 Apr 2025, FPJ).

## How a flat's RR value is computed (valuation guidelines)
- RR rate for flats is per **sq m of built-up area**; built-up taken as carpet + ~20% when only carpet is given (guideline convention; some sites say "carpet" - we label as "साधारण" on screen).
- **Floor premium** (lift buildings; Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Pune, Nashik): up to 4th floor nil; 5th-10th +5%; 11th-20th +10%; 21st-30th +15%; 31st+ +20% (sahakarsutra, indextap, ghar.tv - consistent).
- No-lift buildings: deduction up to 20% for flats above 3rd floor (housing.com).
- **Age depreciation** on market value only: 0-2 yrs nil ... up to 70% for 60+ yr permanent structures (housing.com; intermediate bands not verified - not used in voice).

## Worked example used in the script (labelled उदाहरण)
- Vashi, 2 BHK, carpet 650 sq ft -> built-up ≈ 780 sq ft = 72.5 sq m
- 72.5 × 1,03,000 = ₹74.7 lakh; 7th floor +5% -> **≈ ₹78 lakh RR value**
- Agreement value **₹70 lakh**
- Stamp duty (Navi Mumbai, men): 5% + 1% metro cess = 6%. On 78 lakh = ₹4.68 lakh; on 70 lakh = ₹4.20 lakh -> **≈ ₹48,000 extra** because duty is on the higher value. Registration 1% capped at ₹30,000 (above ₹30 lakh).
  - 1% LBT in Navi Mumbai: sources conflict (some say 7%, 2026 guides say LBT not applicable to NMMC) - **not mentioned in voice**.
  - Women sole owner: 1% rebate (5% total).
- Income tax: tolerance band 10% (since 1 Apr 2021). 78 / 70 = 1.114 > 1.10 -> **both** sides taxed on the ₹8 lakh gap: seller under 50C (deemed sale price 78 lakh for capital gains), buyer under 56(2)(x) (8 lakh taxed as "income from other sources"). From 1 Apr 2026 the Income-tax Act 2025 renumbers these as s.78 and s.92(2)(m) (taxguru) - screen footnote only.
- If agreement were ₹72 lakh: 78/72 = 1.083 < 1.10 -> no income-tax hit, only stamp duty on 78 lakh.

## Why RR matters (script points)
1. Stamp duty + registration base (higher of the two)
2. Income tax trap for both buyer and seller beyond 10%
3. Bank loan valuation often references it; property tax (capital value) in some cities
4. Builder cannot register below RR; a "cheap" deal below RR is a red flag (cash component)
5. RR frozen for 2026-27 -> check your zone before buying
