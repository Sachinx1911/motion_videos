[confident] Okay, here's the deal. The Chembur society signed its agreement in twenty thirteen. The builder promised new homes in twenty-four months. Everyone moved out, into rented flats.

[serious] In eight years, all that came up was an empty concrete structure. Then the builder even stopped paying their rent. Over seven crore rupees of rent still due. Those families are still outside today.

[confident] The answer: one line in the agreement. "Ten lakh rupees penalty for every week of delay." Sixty-two weeks, into ten lakh, equals six crore twenty lakh. The Bombay High Court said the builder has to pay.

[serious] And the Chembur agreement? That line was never there.

[punchy] So if your society is going for redevelopment, ask one question before you sign: if there's a delay, how much per week? Comment below, does your agreement have this line or not? And we bring true cases like this every week, so follow.
