# Redevelopment नंतर flat वाटप lottery नेच? (research 31 Oct 2026)
Trigger: Instagram comment by @surekhapatil1672 on our viral redevelopment reel (4 likes, 2 "same question" replies): "मी 5 व्या मजल्यावर, lottery मध्ये पहिला मजला आला तर? lottery बंधनकारक आहे का?" -> answer as "एका follower ने विचारलं" (no name in video), post as Reply-with-Reel.

## Verified (2+ sources)
- 79A directive 3 Jan 2009 (No. CHS 2007/CR554/14-C), clause 11: after OC, flats "as far as possible ... floor-wise" as per present position; if lots necessary, developer arranges draw on completion, in presence of Registrar's representative, video recorded. [Scribd 2009 copy; consumerresources.in; realestatelawjournal.in]
- 79A directive 4 Jul 2019, clause 11: same - "allotted as far as possible by the present floor position and if a draw of lots becomes necessary ... upon completion of the building ... in the presence of Registrar's Representative and these proceedings should be video recorded". [maharashtrahousingandbuildinglaws.com; redevelopmentofhousingsociety.com; csminfraprojects.com]
- 30 Sep 2026 GR under 79A replaced the 2019 directions (Prahaar, eSakal, Loksatta via our reel_redevelopment/research.md).
