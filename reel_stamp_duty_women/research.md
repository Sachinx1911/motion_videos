# Women 1% stamp duty concession - Maharashtra (research 15 Oct 2026)
- Announced 8 Mar 2021 (Women's Day), effective 1 Apr 2021; Revenue & Forest Dept order 31 Mar 2021 (Mudrank-2021/UOR.12/CR.1071M-1 (Policy)).
- Concession: 1% less stamp duty for women buyers. Examples: Mumbai 6% men / 5% women; Pune, Thane, Nagpur 7% men / 6% women (secondary guides). Registration fee 1%, capped Rs 30,000 (no women concession).
- Only RESIDENTIAL: flat, bungalow, row house, independent house (conveyance or agreement to sell). Not commercial, industrial, land.
- Buyer must be woman alone or with ONLY female co-owners. Adding husband/any male co-owner -> normal rate (most sources; sarkariyojana.com claims woman-first joint ok - disputed, so script says male co-owner = no concession and "confirm with sub-registrar").
- 15-year no-sale-to-male condition (2021) REMOVED by amended order 26 May 2023 (announced 31 May 2023, Fadnavis). Now she can sell anytime to anyone.
- No refund for duty already paid before the order.
- Example: Rs 50 lakh home -> 1% = Rs 50,000 saved.
Sources: piramalrealty.com, businesstoday.in (1 Jun 2023), india.com, mchi.net, vlawpartners.com, e-stampdutyreadyreckoner.com, cleartax.in (2026 rates), readnmore.in, sarkariyojana.com (dissent). Verify current rates on igrmaharashtra.gov.in.
