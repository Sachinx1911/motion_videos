# ताबा उशिरा: RERA कलम 18 व्याज (research 3 Nov 2026)
- RERA s.18(1): possession not given as per agreement date -> allottee may (a) withdraw: refund of amount paid + interest (+ compensation), or (b) stay: interest for every month of delay till possession. [ownproperty.in, rdatax.in, ghar.tv, revaahomes]
- Maharashtra RERA Rules 2017, Rule 18: interest = SBI highest MCLR + 2%; same rate both ways (s.2(za)); on consideration PAID, excluding stamp duty, registration, govt taxes. [MahaRERA orders via squarefeatindia (Kolte-Patil order), ghar.tv]
- Bombay HC Neelkamal Realtors (relied on by MahaRERA): right to interest for delay is unconditional once agreed date lapses. [squarefeatindia Kolte-Patil order]
- SBI MCLR effective 15 Oct 2026: 3-year (highest) 8.95% (was 8.80). [sbi.bank.in MCLR page; Upstox; newsd] -> 8.95 + 2 = 10.95% p.a. SBI may revise ~15 Nov; script says "सध्या साधारण".
- Example: ₹50,00,000 paid x 10.95% = ₹5,47,500 / yr = ₹45,625 / month -> voice "जवळपास पंचेचाळीस हजार".
- MahaRERA SOP 22 Nov 2025: developer must pay compensation within 60 days of a valid order; non-compliance complaint handled in 4 weeks; recovery warrant. [Outlook Money 24 Nov 2025; bricksnwall]
- COVID moratorium 15.03.2020-14.09.2020 (MahaRERA order 14/2020): no s.18 interest for that period. (footer only)
- Interest is simple, month-by-month on each instalment from receipt (ownproperty) - example uses whole ₹50 L as already paid.
