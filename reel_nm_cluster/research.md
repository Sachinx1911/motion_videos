# Navi Mumbai cluster redevelopment: stay lifted (research 27 Oct 2026)

## The news (what actually happened)
- 11 Aug 2026: NMMC general body passed non-official resolution no. 1002 asking for a blanket stay on cluster redevelopment (Urban Renewal Scheme, URS) in the whole NMMC area. [Pudhari 21 Oct]
- 15 Oct 2026: Urban Development Dept (Deputy Secretary Nirmalkumar Chaudhari) ordered NMMC Commissioner: the resolution is void; proposals already notified in the Government Gazette, or pending before the High Power Committee, cannot be stalled by a general body resolution. [Pudhari 21 Oct; FPJ 22 Oct; UNI 21 Oct]
- Directions: C-1 (highly dangerous) building proposals to go for prior govt approval; NMMC to complete a city-wide impact assessment study within 6 months; future proposals checked for effect on roads, water, parking. [Pudhari; FPJ]
- It is NOT a final approval: the 5 proposals still need committee scrutiny and final state approval. [FPJ; UrbanAcres]
- 5 proposals (Ghansoli, Vashi); HPC already approved 3, incl. Simplex settlement Ghansoli and JN-type colony Vashi Sector 9. [FPJ]
- Ghansoli Simplex: 7 societies, 3,260 families, 1-RK flats ~150 sq ft allotted by CIDCO in 2004 to Mathadi workers; promised a little over 500 sq ft. [FPJ]
- Pudhari: stay lifted for 12 societies (Shri Gurudev Datta CHS Vashi + 11), ~3,264 members, 700+ families already on rent; nodes Vashi, Nerul, Kopar Khairane, Ghansoli. (Numbers differ from FPJ -> in video use only FPJ Simplex figure.)
- Politics (Shinde vs Naik) -> NOT in video (independent voice). Mention only the infrastructure concern neutrally.

## Rules: UDCPR 2020, Regulation 14.8 Urban Renewal Scheme (research/udcpr/udcpr.txt lines 20228+)
- 14.8.1(i): cluster minimum 10,000 sq m in non-congested area, 4,000 sq m in congested area; bounded by roads/nallahs etc.; access from >= 18 m road. (8,000 sq m allowed in hardship.)
- 14.8.1(iii): buildings at least 30 years old (authorised or unauthorised), or certified dilapidated / unfit.
- 14.8.4(ii): each eligible residential occupant gets carpet area = area occupied in old building, minimum 30 sq m (= 322.9 sq ft); occupants of AUTHORISED buildings get additional 25% of eligible area.
- 14.8.5(i): for authorised buildings, original area + 25% is free of cost.
- 14.8.6(i): permissible FSI = rehab FSI + incentive FSI, or 4.00, whichever is higher. (News: "FSI up to 4".) Video wording: "चार FSI मिळू शकतो".
- 14.8.11(i): private promoter / CHS / federation needs consent of owners of 51% of the area.
- 14.8.5(iv)(c): rehab tenements not transferable for 15 years without Commissioner's permission; for authorised-building occupants no premium. (Not used in video.)
- Separate route for single CIDCO buildings: Reg. 10.10.2 (+35% carpet, min 300 sq ft, extra 10-20% by plot size) = covered in our 300 sq.ft reel; do not mix.
