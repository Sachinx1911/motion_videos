# CIDCO leasehold -> freehold (research 9 Oct 2026)
- CIDCO acquired Navi Mumbai land (1970s) and gives it ONLY on lease, mostly 60 years (some 99). Flat/building owned, land under building leasehold (society/developer). Nominal annual lease rent. (nobroker lease-deed guide; navimumbairealestate.org)
- Resale: CIDCO NOC + transfer charges (+18% GST) needed; banks/societies insist. Charges revised up from 1 Apr 2025 across nodes (Airoli...Ulwe). Amounts reported inconsistently -> do NOT quote figures. (newsband, urbanacres)
- Oct 2024: CIDCO chairman Sanjay Shirsat announced freehold. Navi Mumbai Citizen Foundation campaign (500 societies).
- July 2025 (Saam TV 11 Jul 2025; constructionworld; therealtytoday): State approved VOLUNTARY conversion of CIDCO residential leasehold plots to freehold. Covers: tender plots, CIDCO housing projects, 12.5% & 22.5% scheme plots. Condition: lease deed executed. Fee = % of ready reckoner rate of land; plus unearned income if lease deed says; extra fixed charge for subsidised/concessional plots. CIDCO committee formed. Apply -> pay -> ownership; Bhumi Abhilekh maintains records.
- After conversion: NO CIDCO transfer charges on future sale/transfer; no CIDCO permission needed. Stamp duty + registration still apply.
- Not automatic. Exact % slabs for Navi Mumbai not confirmed (Lokmat slab example is for Chh. Sambhajinagar) -> don't quote %.
Sources: saamtv.esakal.com (11 Jul 2025), constructionworld.in, therealtytoday.com, housiey.com, propnewz.com (Jul 2026), freepressjournal.in (Oct 2024), newsband.in (Apr 2025 hike).
