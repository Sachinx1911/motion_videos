## Booklet rules (MM/SCH-51/2026-27)
- Bid is per sq m; e-auction increments Rs 1,000 per sq m.
- Close bid = sealed best offer; if bidder doesn't join e-auction, close bid is final.
- Close bid can be withdrawn/resubmitted ONLY ONCE on Final Submission page; after that no withdrawal, no EMD refund request.
- Bid valid 90 days / 3 months from opening; cannot be revoked; withdrawal after opening -> EMD forfeited (clauses 4, 5, 25).
- Clause 34: "If any bidder submits unrealistic erroneous bid mistakenly" and gives representation with satisfactory explanation -> EMD refunded, but 3% of EMD + taxes forfeited as admin charges, on approval of VC & MD; their decision binding. (For plot 1&2: 3% of 27.5 lakh = ~Rs 82,552.)
- Fewer than 3 bids -> CIDCO decides; EMD of those bidders held till decision.
## Lessons (buyer side)
1. Rate is per sq m, not total. 2. Check zeros/commas before final submit. 3. Withdraw/resubmit only once. 4. EMD can be forfeited; clause 34 is discretionary, not automatic. 5. Read the booklet.
