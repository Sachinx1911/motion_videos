# Panvel TDR vs Premium FSI case - research (9 Oct 2026)

- Rule: UDCPR clause 10.16 (notice 15 Mar 2024, notification 7 Oct 2024) - in Panvel MC area, 75% of TDR component (Table 6-G col 5) can be used by paying premium at 60% of ASR land rate; remaining 25% must be TDR. Temporary till Panvel DP sanctioned. (Also in our UDCPR summary_C: 10.16 inserted 7 Oct 2024.)
- Origin: CREDAI BANM-Raigad letter 28 Apr 2023 citing TDR shortage in CIDCO areas; Panvel MC opposed it by letter 5 Sep 2023.
- Petitioners: farmer Pawan Rajaramrao Kadam (WP 6746/2024) + ex-corporator Haresh Keni (Pethali, Taloje Majkur). Argument: farmers get TDR as compensation for land given for roads/hospitals and sell it to builders; premium route kills TDR demand/value; Art. 300A right to property.
- 2024: Bombay HC interim stay (3 Jul 2024, continued Aug 2024).
- 13 Feb 2026: Bombay HC (CJ Shree Chandrashekhar, J Gautam Ankhad) DISMISSED petitions, upheld 10.16 - optional route, no guaranteed TDR value. Builders on CIDCO plots immediately started buying premium FSI.
- 4 May 2026: Supreme Court (J Vikram Nath, J Sandeep Mehta) on Kadam's SLP STAYED premium FSI purchase; status quo till further orders; notice to State, Town Planning dept, Panvel MC, CIDCO, CREDAI; 4 weeks to reply. Next (final) hearing announced for 21 Jul 2026 (Lokmat, 10 May 2026). No report found of the 21 Jul outcome -> treat as pending / stay continuing unless user confirms.
