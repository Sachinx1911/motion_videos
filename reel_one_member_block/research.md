# एक सभासद redevelopment अडवू शकतो का? (research 2 Nov 2026)

## Rules
- GR 30 Sep 2026 (79A): SGM quorum 2/3 of total members; approval by majority but at least 51% of TOTAL members; written consent of those in favour. [our reel_redevelopment/research.md: Loksatta, ZeeBiz/DNA, Pune Pulse]
- UDCPR 10.10.2 clause 3(x) (CIDCO buildings, NMMC): CHS/developer with CIDCO NOC + consent of 70% of members + provision of alternative (incl. transit) accommodation -> "obligatory for all the occupiers/members to participate ... and vacate". [research/udcpr/udcpr.txt; research/redev_rules.md §6]

## Court
- Moon Craft Apartments CHS, Bandra W: 38 members, 32 signed consent declarations by 24 Mar 2026, 6 refused. HC (Justice Amit Borkar), order 18 Jun 2026, s.9 Arbitration Act: directed the 6 to execute declarations, restrained third-party rights; did NOT order immediate vacation (Full IOD pending). Quotes: "redevelopment cannot proceed on the basis of consent of every individual member in every case"; "Otherwise, even a single member may prevent implementation of a project desired by the overwhelming majority." [LiveLaw Biz 19 Jun 2026; Puranik Associates 8 Jul 2026]
- Om Sadan CHS, Borivali: Anil Sunder Jhangiani v. Om Sadan CHS, Comm. Arb. Pet. (L) 31481/2026, Justice Borkar, reported 27 Oct 2026: member who has NOT signed can still get interim protection; cannot be treated in breach / penalised for not vacating before PAAA is executed and registered; undisputed benefits (transit rent, displacement, hardship compensation) to continue; draft PAAA in his name; redevelopment may continue. [LiveLaw Biz 27 Oct 2026] (single report -> voice says "ऑक्टोबरमध्ये")
- Saujanya CHS, Sector 23 Koparkhairane, Navi Mumbai: Baliram Totaram Misal v. Saujanya CHS, WP 15791/2025, Justice Sandeep Marne, 27 Aug 2026: 41 members; challenge to resolutions + development agreement must be one composite civil suit, not split across co-op court and civil court. [FPJ 1-2 Sep 2026; Bar Bulletin; LegalDeli; MumbaiFeed]
- Older: Pantnagar Pearl CHS Ghatkopar (4 of 30 refused, HC: minority cannot obstruct) [Moneylife]; Vasai 11 residents told to vacate in 15 days (Aug 2025, single source) - not used.
