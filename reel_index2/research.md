# Index II (सूची क्र. 2) - काय आणि online कसा काढायचा (research 14 Nov 2026)

## What it is
- Registration Act 1908, s.55: every registration office keeps 4 indexes; Index II = property + transaction particulars; Maharashtra extract = "सूची क्र. 2". [bhume.in]
- One-page summary of a registered document prepared by the Sub-Registrar; who sold/mortgaged what, to whom, for how much, when. [bhume Marathi summary; thanerealestatenews]
- Fields: document type (sale, agreement, gift, mortgage, lease), consideration, market value (govt valuation), property description (village, survey/CTS/plot, flat/floor), area, parties, execution + registration date, doc number, stamp duty, registration fee, SRO. [bhume.in]
- Uses: bank home loan, title checks year by year, mutation/property tax name change. [bhume; thanerealestatenews]
- NOT proof of ownership; shows a registration happened. Online printout = information only; banks/courts usually want certified copy from SRO. [bhume.in; 1acre]

## Online free search (official site checked 14 Nov 2026)
- freesearchigrservice.maharashtra.gov.in (linked from igrmaharashtra.gov.in "eSearch Free Service"); no login, no fee. [official page; bhume; housepartner]
- Tabs exactly: "मिळकत निहाय/Property Details", "दस्त निहाय/Document Number". [official page]
- Property Details fields: सन/Year (1985-2026), जिल्हा/District, गावाचे नाव भरा (English), गाव निवडा, मिळकत क्रमांक (SurveyNo./CTSNo./MilkatNo./GatNo./PlotNo.), captcha. [official page]
- Area tabs (Mumbai / Urban areas in rest of Maharashtra / Rest of Maharashtra) [bhume, mahasuchi]
- Years: Mumbai city + suburban from 1985; 1985-2002 only selected offices; rest roughly from 2002. One year per search. [official page; bhume]
- Official note: "check just before transactions to prevent frauds". [official page]
- Paid eSearch (esearchigr.maharashtra.gov.in) needs login + fee; fee amounts not confirmed -> NOT in voice.
- Offline certified copy fee: sources differ (₹25 + ₹5/page; ₹25 digital / ₹50 paper after Aug 2026) -> NOT in voice.
