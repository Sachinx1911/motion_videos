# CIDCO 60-year lease - ₹177 cr Nerul plot (research 25 Oct 2026)
## Confirmed (FPJ 16 Jul 2026, Construction World 21 Jul, Business Standard 16 Jul, CRE Matrix)
- Plot 3, Sector 52A, Nerul; 5,785.78 sq m (~1.4 acre); lessee Today Royal Developers; lessor CIDCO.
- One-time lease premium Rs 177.04 crore; lease 60 years; annual lease rent Rs 100 (nominal); stamp duty Rs 7.96 crore; lease deed registered 10 Jul 2026; mixed use (residential + commercial); base FSI 1.5. ~Rs 3.06 lakh/sq m (calc).
- Comparable: Godrej Properties, 3 parcels 6.54 acres Kharghar, 60-yr lease, Rs 717 crore (Mar 2025).
- CIDCO leases under Navi Mumbai Disposal of Land (Amendment) Regulations 2008 (earlier 1975). Society lease = 60 years; CIDCO retains ownership of plot. Some special cases 99 years.
- Flat buyer owns the flat; land under building is leasehold (society/developer). Check remaining lease period + dues before buying.
- Transfer charges on resale (NOC); HC ruling ~9 Oct 2026: societies with lease deeds before 8 Feb 1990 (no transfer-charge clause) cannot be charged (Man Mandir, Surya societies; Rs 24.58 lakh demand quashed; Navi Mumbai Co-op Housing Federation).
- Freehold conversion (state approval Jul 2025): voluntary, one-time fee tied to ready reckoner, ~3,500+ societies eligible; after conversion no transfer charges; records to Land Records dept. Background: SC 2022 Aspi Chinoy; Federation campaign; Shirsat announcement late 2024.
- Redevelopment on CIDCO lease needs CIDCO prior written permission (Vashi/Nerul societies 2020).
