#B01 Photorealistic architectural visualisation, golden hour. Low 3/4 view from the opposite road corner of a new 12-storey residential building on a small corner plot in Navi Mumbai, India. Stilt parking at ground level with slim concrete columns and a glass entrance lobby with a wooden canopy. Cream textured plaster walls, warm walnut-wood vertical strips between apartments, projecting balconies with glass railings and thin white sunshade bands over every window. The 8th floor is an open refuge floor with no glass. A tilted wooden crown on the roof with a warm LED strip, a rooftop swimming pool and small terrace garden. Compound wall with a wooden gate and a security cabin, 6 m wide driveway, lawn strip with shrubs and young trees. A 15 m wide road in front with a zebra crossing, footpath, street lights and palm trees; plain neighbouring buildings fade into haze. Warm low sun from the left, long soft shadows, glass reflecting the orange sky, two cars on the road, two people on the footpath. 35 mm lens, building fills the upper two-thirds of the frame. No text, no logos, no signboards.
