# UDCPR-2020 (30 Jan 2025) - Part E: Ch 14.6-15 (book pp. 289-362)
Covers SRA Pune/PCMC (14.6), SRA other MCs (14.7), Urban Renewal / cluster (14.8), eco-tourism, IT township, logistics, Ch 15. (TOD is in part D.)
Unclear: corpus slab 5%/7% around 45 m; lease Rs 1001 per 4000 sq.m; 14.7.8 table rebuilt; URS cut-off = date of sanction (no calendar date).

## 14.6 SRA Pune / PCMC
- Not in-situ: hill slopes, green belt, river/nallah beds, blue line, canal banks, layout open spaces, hazardous land -> relocated.
- Only SRA-registered developers. Only actual occupant eligible; flat in joint names of dweller + spouse.
- Free flat 27.88 sq.m (300 sq.ft) carpet incl. balcony, bath, WC.
- Protected occupier (there on 1 Jan 2000) -> free; non-protected (1 Jan 2011) -> pays GR cost; others -> PMAY.
- 10-year sale/rent bar (heirs exempt); after 10 yrs transfer at 25% ASR premium; breach -> cancelled + lifetime ineligible.
- 51% consent; owner first preference; else 90-day window for dwellers.
- Voluntary land to SRA -> TDR 1.0x (non-congested) / 1.5x (congested).
- Eligibility list in 90 days; RERA compulsory for sale building.
- Shop before 2000: actual area or 300 sq.ft, whichever less; home+shop -> buy up to 6 sq.m shop at construction cost.
- Clearance: 30 days to vacate; non-joiners lose priority; not joined by building permission -> lost permanently; not shifting within 30 days of lottery -> lost.
- Transit 180 sq.ft or rent.
- FSI 4.00 or rehab+sale whichever more; density min 450/ha (not below 360), max 1440.
- Slum TDR released 25% plinth / 35% RCC / 30% OC / 10% conveyance.
- Sale incentive R = 2.8 - 0.3 x ((flat rate / construction rate) - 2), between 1.5 and 3.0; +20%/+30% for dense slums; +10% for slum >=1 ha contiguous.
- Corpus Rs 40,000/flat or 3-7% of construction cost; FD 10 years; interest to society.
- Ceiling 2.75 m; stair 1.5 m; lifts none up to G+4, 1+1 up to G+9, 2+1 up to 16, 2+2 above; parking 1 two-wheeler per flat; developer gives 5-yr AMC + 5-yr insurance.
- Welfare centre + balwadi 27.88 sq.m each per 100 families; health post + police chowky if >500 tenements; 2.5% rehab area society shops.
- Society registered within 60 days of CC; stamp duty fully exempt.
- Lottery with 7 days notice, video-recorded; widows & families with disabled choose first.
- Govt land premium 25% ASR; lease 30 yrs at Rs 1001.

## 14.7 SRA other MCs
- Free 27.88 sq.m; female-headed & differently-abled first; owning any flat in MC area -> ineligible; one-third women on committee; 10-yr bar.
- SRA decides in 60 days else deemed.
- Density min 650/ha (not below 500).
- Free shop up to 225 sq.ft; +5% commercial BUA for society (+5% more if NGO/govt).
- No lift required up to G+5. Corpus Rs 40,000/flat.
- Sale incentive table 1.50-3.00 by LR/RC and plot size.

## 14.8 Urban Renewal (cluster) - all MCs
- Min area 10,000 sq.m non-congested / 4,000 sq.m congested; 18 m+ road; buildings >=30 yrs or dilapidated; slums <=25%.
- Rehab = existing carpet, min 30 sq.m; authorised-building residents +25% extra, free.
- Unauthorised/slum occupants pay: first 30 sq.m free, 30-50 at construction cost, >50 at market rate (Table 14-X).
- FSI = rehab + incentive or 4.00 whichever higher; incentive 1.75-3.00.
- >51% area holders become Implementation Agency within 3 months; under-30-yr authorised buildings need 70% consent; dissenters <30% compensated/acquired.
- Land owners: 4 irrevocable options - ASR value +100% solatium +12% interest / TDR / built-up area / developed plot 50% at FSI 1.1.
- No resale for 15 years; illegal transfer double premium + 12%.
- 10% open space + 10% amenity; heritage Grade I/II untouched.

## Others
- 14.9 Eco-tourism near sanctuaries: min 1 ha, 10% built-up, G+1, 9 m; >=50% renewable energy; plastic not recycled -> closed in 48 hrs.
- 14.10 IT township: min 10 acres; FSI 2.5 big cities / 2.0 / 1.0 agri; 50% IT; completion 7.5-10 yrs.
- 14.11 Logistics park: up to 200% extra FSI; premium 0/10/15%.
- 15.1 Quarry: 200 m from 30 m road/railway/settlement; 500 m if blasting; 1-yr permit, max 3 yrs.
