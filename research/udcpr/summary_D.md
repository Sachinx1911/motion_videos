## Ch 11 Reserved sites / TDR / RCC
- Accommodation Reservation (Table 11-A): owner hands over plot + built amenity free, keeps full FSI + TDR of ENTIRE plot on rest. Garden/playground: hand over 70%, build on 30% with full potential; handed garden >=1000 sq.m.
- Only Authority acquires: stadium, cremation/burial, slaughter house, STP, WTP, water tank.
- Residential EWS/LIG reservation: tenements <=30 sq.m carpet, or hand over 40% land.
- Hand-over % [reconstructed]: A/B/C MCs commercial/health/etc 40% land / 50% built; education 40/50; bus stand 50/20; truck terminus 40/10.
- Composite building premium 40% ASR. OC only after amenity handed over; Authority to open to public within 3 months. No cap on FSI+TDR on remaining plot. After OC non-reserved part deemed residential/commercial.
- 11.2 TDR: DRC is transferable negotiable instrument; usable only within same Authority. Generation: 2x land (non-congested), 3x (congested); 50% in CRZ/hazard zones; biodiversity park 8%; 1.85x/2.85x without levelling & 1.5 m wall. DRC within 90 days.
- Amenity TDR = construction cost (PWD DSR) / ASR land rate x 1.35.
- Utilisation X = (Rg/Rr) x Y (generating-year ASR).
- 30-50% of TDR loading must be slum/URT/amenity TDR.
- TDR not usable: agri/green/NDZ zones, blue line, CRZ, Koregaon Park (Pune).
- Transfer needs registered agreement + Authority endorsement on original DRC. No infrastructure charges on TDR use.
- No TDR: compensation paid, award declared, water bodies, own layout RG, State Govt land (except long lease >30 yrs -> 90%).
- 11.3 RCC: money-value certificate for property tax, premium, dev charges; no interest; transferable.

## Ch 13 Special provisions
- 13.1 Barrier-free: public buildings on plots > 2000 sq.m (NOT residences). Ramp 1:12, >=1.8 m wide, <=9 m; door >=900 mm; threshold <=12 mm; 2 accessible car spaces within 30 m; accessible toilet 1.5 x 1.75 m, door opens outward; lift door open >=5 s.
- 13.2 Solar water heater / rooftop PV mandatory for plots > 4000 sq.m; >=25% of roof; roof load >=50 kg/sq.m.
- 13.3 Rainwater harvesting mandatory for plots >= 500 sq.m + society layouts; society maintains; levy up to Rs 1000/yr per 100 sq.m built-up; RWH not in FSI; two 100 mm downpipes per 100 sq.m roof.
- 13.4 Grey water: >=100 flats (150 EWS/LIG), layouts >=10,000 sq.m; only flushing/gardening/car wash; sale agreement must say test every 6 months; 5% property tax rebate; fine Rs 2,500 + Rs 100/day; not operated Rs 300/day + water cut.
- 13.5 Wet waste on site for complexes > 4000 sq.m built-up and 3-star+ hotels.
- 13.6 (10 Oct 2024) Buildings >10,000 sq.m or >1000 persons: fire tower; tanks every 65 m for 90 m+; electrical inspection every 5 yrs; OC withdrawal / power cut.
