## Ch 7 Higher FSI
- Table 7-A premiums (% ASR): pre-primary/primary school 5%; other education 10% charitable / 15% private; hospitals same, max 3.00 on 18 m+; star hotels 20%; religious 15%; EWS/LIG basic shelter FSI 2.5, no premium; hostels unit max 17 sq.m, rental only.
- 7.3 Govt staff quarters FSI 3.00 (18 m+ road, 4000 sq.m+).
- 7.4 MHADA: FSI 3.00; society redevelopment rehab = carpet + 35% (min 35 sq.m) + 15-45% by plot size; incentive 40-70% (LR/RC); balance FSI society:MHADA 30:70 to 45:55; MHADA itself +15%; 51% consent binds all; corpus fund; slum pockets 300 sq.ft.
- 7.5 Protection of FSI: redevelopment gets greater of today's FSI or FSI already consumed (incl. TDR/premium).
- 7.6 Old/dangerous buildings or ANY building > 30 years old:
  - 7.6.1 Societies/apartments: existing FSI + 30% of existing BUA or 15 sq.m per flat, whichever more; buy premium/TDR up to max; NOT for bungalows; flats < 27.87 sq.m get min 27.87 sq.m (300 sq.ft) free of premium; only when existing members rehoused.
  - 7.6.2 Tenanted: + 50% incentive on rehab area; all tenants rehoused.
  - Notes: 51% agreement on stamp paper; corpus fund for 10 years maintenance; fire-gutted exempt from 30-yr rule.
- 7.7 EWS/LIG on private land: all flats <=50 sq.m -> max potential; 40% <=30 sq.m; one per family.
- 7.8 IT/data centres: MMR/PMR premium 10% (25% CBD); FSI up to 3/3.5/4 by road 12/18/27 m.
- 7.10 Green building: GRIHA 3/4/5-star or IGBC Silver/Gold/Platinum -> 3% / 5% / 7% extra FSI on basic; penalty 2x ASR land cost if rating not achieved at OC.
- 7.11 Public toilet -> amenity TDR + 50% incentive.
- 7.13 Commercial FSI up to 5.0 in CBD/commercial at 50% ASR premium.

## Ch 8 Parking
- Car 2.5 x 5.0 m (50% may be 2.3 x 4.5); scooter 1 x 2 m; stilt 2.4 m.
- Table 8-B residential: >=150 sq.m 2 cars + 1 scooter; 80-150: 1 car + 1 scooter; 40-80: 1 car per 2 flats + 2 scooters per 2; 30-40: 1 car per 2 flats (scooter unclear); <30: no car, 2 scooters per 2 flats; +5% visitor.
- City factor (Table 8-C): Pune/PCMC/Thane 1.0; Nagpur/Nashik 0.9; other MMR & CIDCO 0.8; other MCs 0.7; D-class MC & A-class council 0.6; B/C councils 0.5; NP/RP 0.4. Factor not applied to two-wheelers in residential.
- Lock-up garages count in FSI. Parking >50% above norm charged 10% ASR. Bungalow plots <=300 sq.m no separate parking. Bus bay for >500 flats. Plots <=1000 sq.m may use 2 car lifts.

## Ch 9 Parts of building
- 9.1 Plinth >=30 cm.
- 9.2/9.3 NO minimum room or kitchen size - "as per requirement and convenience of owner".
- Table 9-A ceiling: habitable 2.75-4.5 m; AC room 2.4 m; shops 3.0 m; beam headroom 2.4 m; kitchen 2.75 m.
- 9.4 Bath 1.0 x 1.2 m; WC 0.9 x 0.9 m; combined 1.5 sq.m; height 2.1 m.
- 9.5 Loft max 25% of room, 1.5 m high, 2.1 m clear below.
- 9.7 Mezzanine up to 50% of room, counts in FSI.
- 9.9 Private garage counts in FSI. 9.10 Top terrace cannot be sub-divided; common access only.
- 9.11 Basement storage/lockers count in FSI; parking free.
- 9.14 Balcony 1.0-2.0 m wide; can be enclosed at permission stage if depth <=1/3 room depth.
- 9.20 Windows >=1/10 of floor area; not lit beyond 7.5 m.
- 9.26 Compound wall front 1.5 m (2.4 m if top 0.9 m open); gates must open inward.
- 9.27 Lift: 1 above 15 m, 2 above 24 m; senior-citizen housing lift at any height; adding a floor doesn't need lift extension.
- Table 9-G stairs: house up to G+2 0.75 m; residential up to 15 m 1.0 m; 15-24 m 1.2 m; >24 m 1.5 m; riser <=19 cm, tread >=25 cm, max 15 risers/flight.
- 9.29 Refuge area above 24 m (15 sq.m or 0.3 sq.m/person), then every 15 m; fire lift from 15 m.
- 9.31 >6 flats -> ground-floor entrance lobby >=9 sq.m; 100+ flats -> ~20 sq.m gym/creche/office, 12 sq.m drivers' room, servants' toilet.

## Ch 10 City-specific
- Pune: Koregaon Park - G+1, 1/3 coverage, 2 flats/floor, no subdivision; 21 m cap Parvati/Chatushrungi; no construction 75 m ARDE / 500 yd HEMRL.
- Thane 10.2.10: redevelopment incentive 50% of BUA or 15 sq.m/flat (vs 30% statewide); 70 m buildings on 9-12 m roads (widen to 12 m); parking factor 0.8; high-rise threshold 25 m; margin 6 -> 3 m for plots <=1500 sq.m; 100 m no-build Air Force station.
- Nagpur commercial basic FSI 2.0/2.5; residential plots >=1000 sq.m basic 1.25.
- Nashik cycle track 3 m of 6 m nallah margin.
- Vasai-Virar low-density FSI 0.5. Ulhasnagar max FSI 4.0.
- Navi Mumbai CIDCO redevelopment (10.10.2): buildings >30 yrs "irrespective of dilapidation"; rehab carpet + 35% (min 300 sq.ft) + 10-20%; commercial +20%; incentive 70-100%; 70% consent binds all; corpus fund.
- Bhiwandi affordable unit 25 sq.m; 50% via MHADA lottery.
- Panvel (7 Oct 2024): 75% of TDR can be premium FSI at 60% ASR.
