# UDCPR-2020 (30 Jan 2025) - Part A: front matter + Ch 1-3 (book pp. 1-58)
Sanctioned 2 Dec 2020 (MRTP Act s.37(1AA)(c), 20(4)); 53 later notifications up to 27 Nov 2024.

## Applicability (1.1, p.1)
NOT applicable: MCGM (Mumbai) and authorities within it, MIDC, NAINA, JNPT, hill-station councils, Chikhaldara, MoEF eco-sensitive zones, Lonavala. English prevails over Marathi (1.9(vii), p.19).

## Definitions (1.3)
- Carpet area (def.25, p.4): net usable area excl. external walls, shafts, exclusive balcony/verandah/terrace; incl. internal partition walls (RERA style).
- Built-up (def.21), FSI (def.63) = P-line area / net plot area; basic / premium FSI (def.17).
- High-rise >= 24 m (def.74). Special building > 24 m or non-residential >= 500 sq.m per floor; residential up to 24 m with <=750 sq.m/floor + sprinklers not special (def.93(xiv)).
- Home occupation: family only, no hired labour, no display, no eating place (def.75).
- Painting, plastering, retiling, white-washing are NOT "alteration" (def.2).

## Ch 2 Permission
- 2.1.1 Even demolition needs permission.
- 2.1.2 No permission: window safety grills; rooftop solar base <=1.8 m (with structural cert); sample flat/site office; light partitions in commercial; plots <=150 / 150-300 sq.m via Appendix K; up to 8 agro-tourism rooms (App. K-2).
- 2.1.6 Temporary structures max 6 months at a time, 1 year total.
- 2.1.7 Repairs (doors/windows same place, strengthening): intimation only.
- 2.2.3 7/12 or property card not older than 6 months; revised permission needs buyers' consent per RERA.
- 2.2.7 Plans must show carpet area of every flat + balcony; structural & earthquake cert above G+2 / stilt+2.
- 2.2.10 If architect/engineer leaves, work stands suspended.
- 2.2.12 Scrutiny fee Rs 5 / 4 / 2 per sq.m by city category.
- 2.2.13 No development charge on compound wall, internal repairs without extra FSI.
- 2.2.14 Premium instalments at 8.5%: 10% + 4x22.5% (<70 m) or 20% at CC + 80% at OC; OC in proportion to payment.
- 2.3(vi) Zone line splits plot -> zone covering >50% applies. 2.4 No relaxation in road setback, FSI, parking.
- 2.6.1 Decision within 60 days of resubmission. 2.6.2 DEEMED PERMISSION after 60 days if fully compliant; authority replies in 15 days else CC in next 15 days; action against officers.
- 2.6.4 Plans on website till 1 month after last OC.
- 2.7.1 CC valid 4 years aggregate, renewed yearly (max 3 renewals); no renewal once plinth reached.
- 2.7.2 Group housing internal roads maintained by society.
- 2.8.3 Display board: names, survey no., permission no./date, permitted built-up area, RERA no., QR code.
- 2.8.4 Plinth: ~10% cases inspected.
- 2.8.5 Internal changes within a flat/shop not violating FSI are NOT unauthorised.
- 2.10 OC within 21 days of completion cert else deemed; deemed OC issued within 15 days; special buildings need CFO.
- 2.11 Part OC with indemnity. 2.14 Offences: MRTP s.52-54; licensed persons debarred district-wide; no fine amounts stated. 2.15 Permission by false statement revoked, no compensation.

## Ch 3 Land development
- 3.1.1 Not buildable: entire site within 6 m nallah / 15 m river; slope >1:5; no access; within blue line; entire site within 50 m of wetland.
- 3.1.2 Electric line clearance (vertical/horizontal): LT 2.5/1.2 m; up to 33 kV 3.7/2.0 m; +0.3 m per extra 33 kV; incl. balconies.
- 3.1.3 Blue line = prohibited; blue-red = plinth 0.45 m above red line; FSI usable on rest.
- 3.1.4 Railway NOC within 30 m. 3.1.7 Prison 150/100/50 m. 3.1.9 Airport: no slaughterhouse/garbage within 10 km; >150 m height needs civil aviation. 3.1.12 Lake 100 m (RP).
- 3.2 Access 6 m wide up to 75 m, 9 m up to 150 m; congested >=4.5 m.
- 3.3.2 Layout roads 9/12/15 m by length (150/300 m); group housing 7.5 m upward.
- 3.3.5 Lane <4.5 m congested -> boundary 2.25 m from centre line.
- 3.3.7 Neglected private street repaired by Authority at owners' cost.
- 3.3.8 Default ROW: NH 60 m, SH 45 m, MDR 24 m, ODR 18 m, village road 15 m.
- 3.3.9 Special building: 12 m road, 6 m motorable margins for 45-tonne fire engine, gate 6 m.
- 3.4.1 Recreational open space 10% for layouts >= 0.4 ha (4000 sq.m); 3.4.2 deemed vested in society, transfer at Re 1, cannot be sold/leased; 3.4.3 cannot be deleted/reduced; rearrange only with consent, not after 4 years; 3.4.6 min dimension 10 m; 3.4.7 allowed: club house, gym, yoga, library, swimming pool (max 15% built-up, 10% ground, 2 storeys); utilities need society consent; no detached toilet block.
- 3.5.1 Amenity space 5% for land >= 20,000 sq.m (nil below).
- 3.6 Substation 5x5 m for plot > 2000 sq.m.
- 3.7 Min plot 30 sq.m (residential/commercial), 20 sq.m EWS, industrial 300 sq.m.
- 3.8 Inclusive housing: only MCs with 10 lakh+ population; projects >=4000 sq.m -> 20% for EWS/LIG 30-50 sq.m; MHADA list within 6 months; price ASR construction cost + 25%; OC of rest withheld; exempt: bungalows, CHS redevelopment, PMAY etc.; voluntary -> +25% FSI.
- 3.9 FSI on net plot area. 3.10 Hand over DP site -> its FSI on rest. 3.11 Relocate reservation within 500 m, once.
- 3.13 Cycle track: 3 m of 6 m nallah margin; owner gets FSI + 35% TDR.
