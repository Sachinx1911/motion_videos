# UDCPR-2020 (30 Jan 2025) - Part F: Appendices (book pp. 363-558)

Readable: book pp. 363-405 (App. A-1 to L). App. M (pp. 407-419) and pp. 420-558 are scanned images (contents unknown). From PDF p. 469 printed page = label + 4.

## Structure
- A-1 (363-371) Building permission application: plans in 4 sets; 10 documents; Particulars form; Proforma-I FSI statement.
- A-2 (372-377) Layout / sub-division application.
- B (378) Supervision form + structural engineer certificate.
- C (379-386) Licensed personnel: who can sign what, licence 3 yrs, duties of owner/officers, accident fact-finding committee, PRAPTRA-1 structural adequacy cert.
- D-1 (387) Sanction + CC: valid 1 year if work not commenced; no occupation till OC.
- D-2/D-3 (388-389) Tentative/final layout approval; recreational open space vests in plot holders/society, not developer.
- E-1/E-2 (390-391) Refusal must cite regulation number.
- F (392) Plinth completion intimation (intimation, not approval).
- G (393) Completion certificate: 3 sets of plans; internal changes allowed if no FSI violation; premium fully paid.
- H (394) Full / part OC. I (395) OC refusal ("not as per sanctioned plans"). J (396) Part-OC indemnity on stamp paper.
- K (397-398) Risk-based approval: Low risk <=150 sq.m., Moderate 150-300 sq.m.
- K-1 (399) Intimation form for plot <=150 sq.m.: "Please treat this intimation as permission".
- K-2 (400-401) Agro-tourism up to 8 rooms.
- L (402-405) Heritage listing card.

## Key facts
- App. K Low Risk (plot up to 150 sq.m.): G+2 or Stilt+3, residential (+ ground-floor shop); plot in sanctioned layout / Gunthewari regularised / independent 7/12 or property card; NOT unauthorised sub-division. Receipt of application + 5 docs + fees "shall be treated as permission for development". No plinth checking.
  5 docs: ownership doc; layout copy or measurement plan; P-line floor-wise plan + Proforma-I FSI; development charges + labour cess receipt; licensed person's certificate of UDCPR compliance.
- App. K Moderate Risk (150-300 sq.m.): G+2 / Stilt+3, mixed use allowed; demand note within 10 days; CC within 10 days of payment without scrutiny; OC within 10 days after site inspection (row split inferred - verify). Any deficiency = unauthorised construction. Fast track optional.
- Plot area for permission = minimum of 7/12-CTS area, measurement sheet, actual site (pp. 365, 370).
- Lift to be stated if height > 15 m; inclusive housing 20% of net plot area (Reg 3.8).
- D-1: CC valid 1 year if work not started; set-back land becomes public street; no occupation before OC.
- Who signs: Architect/Engineer - all plans, structural up to 500 sq.m. & 3 storeys/11 m; Supervisor-1 plots up to 500 sq.m.; Supervisor-2 up to 200 sq.m. built-up. Licence 3 yrs; fees Rs 3,000 (Engr/StrE/TP), Rs 1,500 (Supervisors) "for three years" (heading says annual - unclear), +10% every 3 yrs.
- Officers must act on complaints within 15 days; decide within Right to Services Act timelines. Accident committee: primary report 3 working days, final 10.
- Owner responsible for title and for no violation even after OC.
- K-2 Agro-tourism: agricultural zone, G+1, FSI 0.2; up to 0.8 ha = 4 rooms, 0.8-1.6 ha = 6, >=1.6 ha = 8 + 2 dormitories; >8 rooms = commercial. ("min 150 sq.m." room area wording unclear.)
- Unclear: App. K cites "Appendix H" for completion cert (G is completion, H is OC).

## Reel ideas
1. 150 sq.m. plot? अर्जाची पावतीच तुमची बांधकाम परवानगी (App. K Low Risk).
2. लहान plot वर घर बांधायला फक्त 5 कागदपत्रं (K row 9, K-1).
3. 150-300 sq.m.: 10 + 10 दिवसांत CC (Moderate risk).
4. तुमच्या plot चं क्षेत्र 7/12 वरचं नाही - तिन्हींपैकी सर्वात कमी (pp. 365, 370).
5. बांधकाम परवानगी 1 वर्षात संपू शकते (D-1).
6. OC शिवाय घरात राहता येतं का? घराचे 4 दाखले (D-1, F, G, H, I, J).
7. तुमचा नकाशा कोण sign करू शकतो? Architect / Engineer / Supervisor (App. C).
8. शेतात farm stay: फक्त कळवून 8 खोल्या (K-2).
