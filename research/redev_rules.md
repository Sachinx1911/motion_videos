## 1. Which rule applies?
| Case | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Building constructed by CIDCO, 30+ years old OR declared dilapidated by NMMC, redeveloped by CHS / occupiers / CIDCO lessees | Reg. 10.10.2 (single plot / condominium) [11570+] | ✓ |
| Same, but CIDCO itself redevelops (alone or jointly) | Reg. 10.10.2 clause 2.1: rehab +15% more, NO sharing - whole balance FSI to CIDCO for affordable housing [11704] | ✓ |

## 2. Total FSI for CIDCO-building redevelopment - Reg. 10.10.2, Table 10-C [11593]
Total permissible FSI = Table 10-C value OR (Rehab + Incentive), whichever is MORE; on GROSS plot area.
| Plot | Access road | FSI |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 sq m or more | min 15.0 m | 3.00 |
| 1,000 sq m or more | min 9.0 m | 2.00 |
| All other plots with access road below 9.0 m | - | 1.80 or (consumed FSI + 50% incentive), whichever is LESS |
- ⚠ A plot < 1,000 sq m on a road >= 9 m is not covered by the 3 rows in plain text -> label "स्पष्टता हवी" or use rehab+incentive route; ask an architect before using in a video.
- If the above is less than Table 6-G max potential, the difference can be taken by paying premium; 6-G is read one road-step lower, min 12 m road [11605].
- Table 6-G, column 6 (all Municipal Corporations), max building potential incl. in-situ FSI: road 9-12 m 2.00 · 12-15 m 2.25 · 15-24 m 2.50 · 24-30 m 2.75 · 30 m+ 3.00 [7144]. NMMC note: where basic FSI is 1.5 the 6-G max may be exceeded by 0.5 [10.10.1 note 3].
- Ancillary area FSI: up to 60% of the proposed FSI extra, on premium, all buildings (Table 6-G note i) [7160]. -> real construction can be ~1.6 x FSI area. Mention carefully ("premium भरून जास्त बांधकाम").
- Infrastructure charge: 10% of ASR land rate on the extra FSI above basic zonal FSI, paid to NMMC [10.10.2 xii].

## 3. What each existing flat gets - Reg. 10.10.2 clause 2(A) [11621]
- Basic: existing carpet area + 35%, minimum 300 sq ft carpet (27.88 sq m).
- Additional by plot size (Table 10-D): up to 4,000 sq m nil · 4,000 sq m-2 ha +10% · 2-5 ha +15% · 5-10 ha +20% (of existing carpet).
- Cap: total rehab not above the MIG maximum carpet area prescribed by Govt. ⚠ (find current MIG limit).
- Commercial / amenity unit: existing carpet + 20%.
- If CIDCO redevelops (2.1): +15% of existing carpet more.
- Balcony excluded from these carpet figures.

## 4. Incentive FSI - Table 10-E (by Basic Ratio = ASR land rate / ASR RCC construction rate) [11668]
| LR/RC | Incentive (% of rehab area) |
|---|---|
| above 3.00 | 70% |
| 2.00-3.00 | 80% |
| 1.00-2.00 | 90% |
| up to 1.00 | 100% |
Subject to FSI availability and NMMC distribution with CIDCO's prior approval. LR and RC of the approval year.

## 5. Sharing the balance FSI - Table 10-F [11690]
Balance = total FSI - rehab - incentive. Shared as built-up area:
| LR/RC | Society share | CIDCO share |
|---|---|---|
| above 3.00 | 30% | 70% |
| 2.00-3.00 | 40% | 60% |
| 1.00-2.00 | 50% | 50% |
| up to 1.00 | 60% | 40% |
CIDCO's share is handed over free; in NMMC area up to 20% of CIDCO's share (as tenements) goes free to NMMC for project-affected persons.
⚠ Needs node-wise LR (ASR land rate) and RC (ASR RCC construction rate) -> A2 (IGR e-ASR portal). News sources: Vashi residential ₹1,03,000/sq m (FY 2026-27 rates frozen at 2025-26); Vashi land ₹54,700/sq m from one 2025 source only -> NOT verified. RC not found yet.

## 6. Other conditions - Reg. 10.10.2 clause 3 [11716+]
- FSI on whole layout incl. road widening, excluding land for public amenities; amalgamation of societies allowed with 70% consent of leaseholders/occupants (i).
- Road widening to reach Table 10-C road width allowed by handing the strip to NMMC free (ii).
- Density may exceed normal limit in multiple of FSI (iv).
- Residential stays residential; convenience shopping 5% of plot if plot > 1,000 sq m on 20 m+ road, 10% on 30 m+ road (v).
- Compulsory recreational open space ON GROUND, open to sky, over and above podium garden: 10% for plot 2,500-4,000 sq m, 15% above 4,000 sq m (vi). Below 2,500 sq m: not compulsory under this clause.
- 5% of plot surrendered free to NMMC as amenity if plot > 2,500 sq m (or equivalent carpet built and handed over) (vii).
- Whole-layout plan needed at CC stage; phased redevelopment allowed (viii).
- Age proof: assessment record / city survey / sanad if plans missing (ix).
- With CIDCO NOC + 70% members' consent + alternative/transit accommodation, ALL occupiers must participate and vacate (x).
- Corpus fund by developer for maintenance, amount decided by Commissioner (xiii).

## 9. Amenity bands for the series (draft, based on rules above)
