# Plans → 3D: how Navi Mumbai redevelopment buildings are designed and rendered (study, 27 Oct 2026)
Reference set (user's folder `D:\All Site Latest Plans\` + 18 rendered images): Maitri Sanpada, Pride New Panvel, Kamothe plot 13, Kalakar Patrakar CHS, Heritage, Dreamwood, Heights, Anuparva, Matoshree, Kasturi I/II, Vidyadan, Dnyandeep, Sawali, Honey Homes, Ekta Vihar, Swapnpoorti, Tathagat, Paradise.

## 1. A plan set - what sheets exist and in which order
| Sheet | What it fixes | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Basement (often stack parking) | parking count, ramp down | Pride p9, Heights, Dreamwood |
| Ground floor | plot boundary (red line), roads + widths, gates, 6 m driveways round the building, shops on the main-road frontage, entrance lobbies at each lift core, ramp 6 m wide slope 1:8 | Pride p4, Kamothe p3, Kalakar p1 |
| 1st / podium | parking deck OR amenities deck: lawn, pool, kids play, open gym, yoga, pavilion, clubhouse, party hall | Maitri podium, Kalakar p2, Dreamwood aerial |
| 2nd (transition) | first flats + clubhouse/gym on the podium level | Pride p5, Kamothe p5 |
| Typical floor (one drawing for many floors) | the flat stack that repeats; title lists the floors, e.g. "3rd-6th, 8th-10th, 12th-14th" | Pride p8, Kamothe p7, Maitri |
| Refuge floors (separate sheet) | same floor with 1-2 flats replaced by an open REFUGE AREA (Pride: 41.97 + 69.24 sq m) | Pride p7 (7th & 11th), Kamothe p8 (7th & 12th), Maitri (8th & 13th) |
| Top floor / terrace | different flat mix, terrace, LMR (lift machine room), OHT (water tank) | Maitri 15th |
| Elevation / 3D | facade, colours, crown | every project |
=> The building is a STACK: ground (shops/stilt/lobby) → 0-2 parking or podium levels → N typical floors with refuge floors inserted every ~4-5 floors → terrace + crown.

## 2. Typical floor anatomy (the part that repeats)
- **Double-loaded corridor**: a 1.5-2.1 m (5'-7') passage with flats on both sides (Pride "6' wide passage", Maitri "1.50 M wide pass.").
- **Core** at each wing junction: stair + 2-3 lifts (Pride lifts 6'6"×5'6" to 9'6"×6'6"; Maitri passenger 7'×7' + STRETCHER LIFT 8'×7'), lift lobby 7' wide, fire duct.
- **Wings**: plan shape follows the plot - L (Pride, plot triangular), T/Y (Maitri), U/C (Kamothe), rectangle (Dreamwood, Anuparva), courtyard square (Dreamwood New Plan, Pride courtyard render).
- **Flat module** (typical sizes read from plans):
  - 1 BHK ≈ 450-500 sq ft carpet: living 14'6"×9'6", bedroom 10'×10'-11', kitchen 7'6"×7'
  - 2 BHK ≈ 700-920 sq ft carpet (Maitri CA 716 / 738 / 915; sale area ≈ 1.67 × carpet)
  - frontage per flat ≈ 7-9 m on the outer wall
- **Ducts / shafts** (the X-boxes on plans) sit between flats, back-to-back with toilets and kitchens.
- **Chajja** 0.75 m all round the outer wall (window shades); balconies / "dry balcony" at the living room or kitchen.
- **Refuge floor**: 1-2 flats left open on the floor just above 24 m, then roughly every 15 m (UDCPR 9.29.6). Pride 7th & 11th, Kamothe 7th & 12th, Maitri 8th & 13th.

## 3. Ground + podium grammar (what the walkthrough walks through)
- Plot edge = compound wall + gate(s) + security cabin (Honey Homes, Matoshree: classical gate with pillars, jaali screen wall).
- 6 m driveway loop round the building (fire tender access), 3 m side driveways.
- Main-road frontage = row of shops with glass fronts + canopy band (Pride, Heritage, Kasturi I, Dreamwood); the residential entrance lobby is on the side road or a gap in the shop row (Pride "A-wing / B-wing entrance lobby").
- Ramp 6 m wide, 1:8, curving (Pride: down to basement, up to 1st floor) or straight (Kalakar to podium; Heritage render shows the ramp under the podium edge).
- Podium / amenities deck = edge planters + glass railing, wooden deck, pool with loungers and umbrellas, lawn, kids play (rubber flooring, slides), pergola/gazebo, open gym, yoga deck, clubhouse / indoor party hall at the corner (Maitri, Dreamwood, Heritage, Pride courtyard).
- On plots ≤ ~2,000 sq m the pool usually sits on the podium of the parking block (Dreamwood aerial), not on the ground.

## 4. How the plan becomes the 3D facade (the key mapping)
| Plan element | Facade / 3D element | Example |
|---|---|---|
| each flat's outer wall (7-9 m) | one vertical BAY of windows/balconies repeated up the building | every project |
| duct / shaft between flats | recessed dark vertical STRIP (grey or brown) | Pride grey strips, Heritage brown strips |
| living room / balcony bay | projecting FRAME or balcony with glass railing, lit soffit | Kasturi I, Matoshree, Honey Homes |
| chajja 0.75 m | thin horizontal white band over each window | Heritage, Pride |
| stair / corridor end | full-height GLASS strip or louvre strip | Kasturi II, Pride courtyard (right edge) |
| lift core | tallest solid shaft, carries the society name, rises above the roof | Kasturi I tower, Heritage |
| LMR + water tank on terrace | hidden behind a CROWN: frame / pediment / louvres / curved canopy with LED strip | Kasturi (tilted crown), Matoshree & Sawali (classical pediment), Vidyadan (curved canopy), Anuparva (flat band) |
| refuge floor | a floor of open bays (no glass) - visible as a "missing" floor band | Maitri render 8th/13th, our test |
| shops on ground | double-height glazing + canopy fascia band | Pride, Heritage, Kasturi I |
| podium edge | planters + glass railing + horizontal fins/louvres | Dreamwood, Kasturi II |

## 6. Render conventions (how the 3D images are composed)
- Camera: from the road CORNER, low 3/4 angle (eye ~1.6-10 m), 28-35 mm, two facades visible; or aerial 3/4 to show podium amenities.
- Light: golden hour or dusk; interiors warm-lit, LED strip on crown, street lights on; same view as day + night pair (Kasturi I).
- Context: wide road with zebra crossing, moving cars (motion blur), palms + street lights, a few people, neighbouring buildings kept plain/blurred, green lawn foreground.
- Hero rules: building fills 60-75 % of frame height, crown against the brightest sky, ground floor fully lit.

## 7. What we take for our own generator (tools to build)
Inputs: plot polygon + road edges (+widths), setbacks (front 3 m, side/rear 6 m for 30+ yr redevelopment), FSI → total built-up, flats (count × carpet), parking type (stilt / podium / basement), style family A-D.
Steps:
1. Buildable footprint = plot minus margins; choose wing shape (rect / L / T / U) that fits.
2. Corridor along the wing centre; core(s) at wing junctions: stair + 2 lifts (+ stretcher lift if > 24 m).
3. Cut flats along both sides in 7-9 m bays; count per floor; floors = built-up / floor plate (cap by margins/height).
4. Insert refuge floors (first above 24 m, then every 15 m); terrace + LMR + crown.
5. Facade from bays (section 4 mapping) with the chosen style family.
6. Ground: shops on main road if mixed use, else stilt; gate, 6 m driveway, ramp if podium; podium amenities (pool, lawn, play, gym, clubhouse).
7. Camera rig for walkthrough: road → gate → driveway/ramp → lobby → podium amenities → lift (floor counter, refuge marker) → flat interior / balcony view → terrace → aerial orbit → front pull-back (day → night lights on).
