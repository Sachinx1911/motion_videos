# SRS Dwell audience map (27 Oct 2026) - Navi Mumbai / MMR flat owners, society members, first-time buyers
Status: GUESSES from our topic research, not survey data. Replace with real comment questions as they come (add date + reel).

## Biggest problems
1. Old CIDCO buildings (30-40 yrs): leakage, cracks, small 1-RK / 1-BHK, families grown.
2. Redevelopment stuck: committee fights, one member blocks, builder delays, rent stops.
3. Not knowing what area / rent / corpus they are entitled to.
4. Builder agreements full of traps (parking, area, delay penalty).
5. Possession delays, no OC/CC, water shortage in new nodes.
6. CIDCO lease / transfer charges / NOC confusion.
7. Plot / lottery / tender rules too complex; fear of losing EMD.
8. Stamp duty, registration, tax costs higher than expected.
9. Fake promises in brochures (amenities, distance to airport/metro).
10. Not knowing whom to complain to (MahaRERA, registrar, NMMC).

## Common mistakes / myths
- Signing development agreement without checking carpet area in writing.
- Thinking "builder gives free house out of kindness" (incentive FSI is the real reason).
- Paying for open/stilt parking.
- Believing balcony / loading is free area.
- Treating 7/12 name as full title.

## Questions they ask (comment-style)
- माझी building redevelopment / cluster मध्ये येते का? किती sq.ft मिळेल?
- भाडं किती आणि किती महिने? Corpus वर tax?
- एक सभासद नको म्हणाला तर?
- CIDCO transfer charge किती? NOC कधी लागते?
- Lottery / tender मध्ये EMD परत कधी मिळते?

## Scroll-stoppers that worked / to test
- Real number contrast (150 → 500 sq.ft, ₹35 कोटी → ₹2,170 कोटी).
- "हे माहीत नसेल तर पैसे जातील" rights angle (parking, stamp duty).
- Fresh news + what it means for you (stay lifted, new GR).
