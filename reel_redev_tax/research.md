# Redevelopment: rent, corpus, hardship money, new flat - income tax? (research 27 Oct 2026)
Not tax advice. Video says "CA चा सल्ला घ्या".

## 1. New flat received in exchange (ITAT Mumbai)
- Anil Dattaram Pitale vs ITO, ITA No. 465/Mum/2025, order 17-03-2025, bench BR Baskaran (AM) & Sandeep Gosain (JM). [SAG Infotech, Moneylife, FPJ, Angel One, News24, Outlook Money]
- Flat bought FY 1997-98, Mahavir Nagar Tristar CHS (Andheri); new larger flat allotted 26-12-2017.
- AO taxed u/s 56(2)(x): stamp value of new flat ₹25.17 lakh - indexed cost of old ₹5.43 lakh = ₹19.74 lakh as "income from other sources"; CIT(A) upheld.
- ITAT deleted: "extinguishment of old flat and in lieu thereof ... got new flat", "not a case of receipt of immovable property for inadequate consideration" -> 56(2)(x) not applicable.
- Capital gains on the exchange / later sale: NOT decided here; experts say may apply case to case; Sec 54 relief possible. -> video: "पुढे विकलं तर capital gains चा हिशोब".

## 2. Transit / displacement rent
- Smt Delilah Raj Mansukhani v ITO (main precedent), Ajay Parasmal Kothari v ITO (coordinate bench) -> capital receipt. [TaxGuru, Mondaq]
- Dr. Girish Suresh Walavalkar v ITO, ITAT Mumbai, AY 2017-18 (reported 2026): compensation for hardship and displacement = capital receipt; taxability "does not depend upon actual utilisation for alternative accommodation"; addition ₹4,05,896 deleted. [TaxGuru]

## 3. Hardship compensation
- Kunnama V Balakrishna v ITO (AY 2011-12, ₹25,21,508) capital receipt; Krishnakant Sharma v ITO (4 Apr 2025); Subhash Suryakant Phadkar v ITO (₹53.50 lakh) capital receipt, not IFOS. [TaxGuru x3]

## 4. Corpus fund
- ITAT Mumbai (reported 9 Sep 2016): corpus ₹22 lakh for hardship = capital receipt; reason: it reduces cost of acquisition of the asset. [Taxscan]
- Interest earned on corpus kept in FD = taxable income (general rule; NoBroker forum + basic law).

## 5. Caveats
- These are ITAT (tribunal) orders, not Supreme Court; AO still raises additions (all cases above started as additions). Keep agreement + receipts; disclose; CA advice.
- Income-tax Act, 2025 in force from 1 Apr 2026 (FY 2026-27 = "tax year"); CBDT: rewrite "without altering the underlying tax policy"; sections renumbered (e.g. old 54 -> new ~82, sources differ). Screen note only.
- Not in video: 45(5A) JDA debate (contested, applies to land/building owners under JDA; society-member view differs).
