# Builder ने भाडं थांबवलं, building अर्धवट: society चे हक्क (research 5 Nov 2026)

## Case 1: Chembur (main story)
- Maruthi CHS v. Tridhaatu Maruthi Developers LLP, Comm. Arb. Pet. (L) 26942/2026, 2026 LLBiz HC(BOM) 580, Justice Arun R. Pedneker, s.9 Arbitration Act (interim measures). [LiveLaw Biz 19 Oct 2026; FPJ 19 Oct 2026]
- Building "Shobha" (1967), Chembur; 22 members, many senior citizens; vacated May-June 2014. [LiveLaw]
- DA 10 July 2013; residents told they would move back within 24 months. [FPJ]
- Deadline: Jan 2017 (order calc) / Jan 2018 (petition) -> video says only "आठ वर्षं काही प्रगती नाही" (court quote: "failed to make any meaningful progress on the Project for the past eight years"). [FPJ]
- Bare structure up to 10th floor (permission only 8), then abandoned. [LiveLaw; FPJ]
- Transit rent arrears ₹7,35,07,670 as of 7 Apr 2026; statutory dues ₹1,10,76,852. [LiveLaw]
- Show-cause notice March 2026; termination of DA + power of attorney 7 Apr 2026. [FPJ; LiveLaw]
- Court: society may appoint new developer and proceed; developer to pay rent arrears + dues; third-party flat buyers' agreements do not bind society. Order date: one source says 16 Oct 2026 -> voice says "ऑक्टोबरमध्ये". [LiveLaw; ibclaw/substack]
- Developer did not appear, ex parte, amicus appointed. (not in voice)
- Society must send arbitration notice within 30 days, else interim order lapses. (screen note only)

## Case 2: Goregaon (prevention: penalty clause)
- The Jal Ratan Deep CHS, Goregaon W v. builder (not named in video); agreement 2011; clause ₹10 lakh per week of delay; 62 weeks delay; arbitral award ₹6.20 crore; Justice Aarti Sathe upheld award, dismissed builder's petition. [FPJ Marathi 25 Oct 2026; JPN News Oct 2026]
- 62 × 10 lakh = 6.20 crore ✓

## Other similar (not in voice)
- Sangat Sadan CHS v. Guinea Global Next LLP: ₹53.9 lakh rent arrears, developer to vacate site, new developer allowed (early Nov 2026, single source). 
- Wadala 826 flats, 13 years no brick, Justice Marne; developer's s.9 dismissed (Apr 2026).

## Where to complain
- Existing members' rehab/transit rent claims: MahaRERA usually holds them outside RERA (not "allottees") -> civil court / arbitration under DA. Sale-component buyers can go to MahaRERA. [LiveLaw consumer (MahaRERA Pune order); Tata Capital explainer; SquareFeat SRA order; Manish Rajput v. Rajhans Realtors] -> voice: "बहुतेक वेळा".

## Rules
- GR 30 Sep 2026 (79A): bank guarantee in proportion to project cost; alternative accommodation or monthly rent on time till new flat; construction 2 yrs from plinth CC (max 3). [Saam TV; eSakal; our reel_redevelopment/research.md]
- GR does NOT (per sources) say DA is auto-cancelled on delay -> not claimed.
