# Bedroom minimum size - research (UDCPR-2020, amended to 30 Jan 2025)
- Reg 9.2.1: "Size and dimension of habitable rooms shall be as per requirement and convenience of the owner." -> NO minimum area/width for bedroom/living room.
- Reg 9.3.1: kitchen size also "as per requirement and convenience of owner".
- Table 9-A (9.2.2): habitable room height min 2.75 m, max 4.5 m; EWS/LIG max 4.2; air-conditioned room min 2.4 m; kitchen min 2.75 m (9.3.2).
- Reg 9.4.1: bathroom min 1.00 x 1.20 m; WC 0.9 x 0.9 m; combined 1.50 sq.m (min width 1.0 m); height 2.1 m (9.4.2).
- Reg 9.20.1: window/opening area of habitable room & kitchen >= 1/10 of floor area (excluding doors).
- Contrast: NBC 2016 Part 3 (model code, not binding in Maharashtra planning permissions) - habitable room min 9.5 sq.m, min width 2.4 m. Old DC rules of many corporations had 9.0/9.5 sq.m minimum; UDCPR removed it.
- Not applicable: Mumbai (MCGM) - DCPR 2034 applies there.
